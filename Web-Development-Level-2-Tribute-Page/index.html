Who Was Ahmed Zewail?

Ahmed Hassan Zewail was an Egyptian-American scientist and professor who made groundbreaking contributions to the field of chemistry. He was born in Damanhur, Egypt, in 1946 and became one of the world's most respected scientists.

His research established the field of femtochemistry, which studies chemical reactions on extremely short timescales. His pioneering work allowed scientists to observe molecules during chemical reactions.

In 1999, Zewail received the Nobel Prize in Chemistry for his pioneering work in femtosecond spectroscopy.
